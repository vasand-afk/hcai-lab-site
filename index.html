Who We Are

A lab run by a clinician who learned to build, not just prescribe.

Dr. Vasan's HC AI Lab is an independent research and development group building applied artificial intelligence for healthcare — spanning diagnostic imaging, cardiac signal analysis, and the clinical workflow software that determines whether any of it actually reaches a patient.

We are deliberately narrow in method and broad in scope: every model is built for one clinical question at a time, validated against real outcomes before it is called anything more than research-stage, and designed from the start to sit inside how care actually gets delivered — not around it.

01Clinician-first designEvery model starts from a real diagnostic or workflow gap, not an available dataset.
02Task-specific architectureWe research what actually wins for each condition rather than reusing one model everywhere.
03Auditable by constructionMulti-agent pipelines log a decision trail a clinician — or a regulator — can follow.

Our Research

Method over momentum.

We publish our reasoning, not just our results — including the architectures we tried and rejected.

Approach

Task-specific architecture research

Before any new condition, we research what predicate devices and open-source SOTA actually use — and pivot away from our own prior architecture when the evidence says to.

Practice

Agent-checking-agent validation

Our multi-agent pipelines include independent auditor agents that score every extracted rule or drafted claim against its cited source before it reaches a clinician.

Publishing

Open weights & code

Validated model checkpoints and training code are released on GitHub and Hugging Face as each project reaches evaluation.
