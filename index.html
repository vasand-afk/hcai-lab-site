Who We Are

A lab run by a clinician who learned to build, not just prescribe.

Dr. Vasan's Rx is an independent research and development group building applied artificial intelligence for healthcare — spanning diagnostic imaging, cardiac signal analysis, and the clinical workflow software that determines whether any of it actually reaches a patient.

We are deliberately narrow in method and broad in scope: every model is built for one clinical question at a time, validated against real outcomes before it is called anything more than research-stage, and designed from the start to sit inside how care actually gets delivered — not around it.

01Clinician-first designEvery model starts from a real diagnostic or workflow gap, not an available dataset.
02Task-specific architectureWe research what actually wins for each condition rather than reusing one model everywhere.
03Auditable by constructionMulti-agent pipelines log a decision trail a clinician — or a regulator — can follow.

Why now

  • Portfolio, not a point product. Twenty-one shots on goal across imaging, cardiology, and clinical workflow AI — de-risked by breadth, not a single bet on one model clearing FDA review.
  • Clinician-led, not clinician-advised. Every architecture decision is made by a practicing physician who also builds the models — closing the gap between what gets built and what a clinician would actually trust.
  • Real validation discipline. Nothing is called "validated" here until it's been evaluated against a genuinely held-out test set — the same standard investors will see applied to every future model in the pipeline.
  • Workflow, not just diagnosis. Our platform layer (Vasan Health, Healthcare Workflow Orchestrator) means our imaging models have a real destination to deploy into, not just a benchmark to beat.
